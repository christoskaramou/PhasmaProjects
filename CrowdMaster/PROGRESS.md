## Design (v1)
World: Y up, track along +Z, 9 m wide floating slab over a sea that reaches the horizon; skybox + fog; islands/mountains as backdrop.
Crowd = n1 Pawns (power 1), n2 Brutes (power 3, 2 HP, merged from 2 pawns), n3 Giants (power 8, 4 HP, merged from 2 brutes).
 Gates: +N adds pawns (Gate Power upgrade boosts), xN multiplies PAWNS only, -N removes power (pawns first), /N divides pawns only (big units immune).
 Merge Gate (gold): pairs of pawns -> brutes (if no pawns: brutes -> giants). Decision: merge (power up, trap/terrain resistant) vs keep pawns for x gates.
 Enemies: red crowds with badge, drain 1:1 vs power (pawns die first). Captives: grey pawns on track join you. Coins on track.
 Traps: spinner bar, saw lane, hammer, crusher, spike roller, pits / narrow bridges (fall into the sea). Big units take hits (HP) instead of dying.
 Terrain: oil (drift, pawns slip off edges), ice (fast + drift), mud (slow), gravel (slow, small bleed), thorns (bleed pawns). Giants ignore slip/bleed.
 Gate traps: Trick gate (striped posts, sign flips/scrambles near), Timed gate (lamp, toggles), x gate followed by trap, Guarded gate (hazard in front).
 Finale: Trophy Staircase (20 steps x1.0..x5.0, top 4 = trophy steps that give gems; each Giant adds +1 reach step) or Boss (every 3rd level) + castle.
Economy: coins = track coins + finish power*mult + enemies + perfect gates + traps cleared + first clear. Gems = trophy steps, boss kill, perfect run.
 Camp screen between levels: Upgrades (coins): Starting Crowd, Gate Power, Trap Armor, Run Speed, Clash Might, Treasury (income per minute of play, collected in Camp; engine has no wall clock => no true offline income).
 Gem Upgrades: Shield (Space, immune N s), Magnet (save slipping/falling pawns, pull coins+captives), Boss Breaker (hammer units, big boss damage), Lucky Eye (trick gates reveal early).
 Army board: 5x3 merge grid, Recruit Pawn for coins, drag or click two equal units to merge; board army joins every run.
Save: Save/crowdmaster.sav (coins, gems, upgrades, board, best scores, unlocked).
