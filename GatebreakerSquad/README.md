# Gatebreaker Squad

A bright, mobile-style gate shooter for the Phasma Engine (Lua project). Your squad holds the bottom of the screen and fires straight ahead on its own. You only steer left/right, and steering is how you aim (Rockets and allies home in). Enemies, gates, crates, containers and bosses come toward you. Shoot gates to raise their values, free allies, grab weapons, merge troops, then beat the boss.

## Run on Windows (PC)
Location: `C:\Users\Christos\repos\PhasmaProjects\GatebreakerSquad`. The engine checkout is `..\..\PhasmaEngine` (`C:\Users\Christos\repos\PhasmaEngine`) and needs a Release build containing `build*\Release\PhasmaPlayer.exe`. You can override it with `$env:PHASMA_ENGINE`.
- `.\run.cmd`, or `powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run.ps1 [-Api vulkan] [-Display 1]`. This copies the Player into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PhasmaGames\GatebreakerSquad` and points it at `project\Assets`, so the engine build's own `phasma_settings.json` is never touched.
- In the editor, open `project\phasma_project.json`.
- Rebuilding `game.lua` from `src\` uses `tools/build_lua.sh` (bash or Git Bash). The Linux-only box launch, screenshot and mesh-cook scripts are not included in the PC copy.

## Run
- **Phasma Editor/Launcher:** open `project/phasma_project.json` (project "GatebreakerSquad") and press Play. The entry scene loads `Assets/Scripts/game.lua`.
- **Box (headless test):** `tools/box_run.sh` (Xvfb :56, MCP port 9156). Stop it with `tools/box_stop.sh` (`ALL=1` also stops Xvfb).
- MCP: `Assets/Agent/agent_config.json` → port **9156**.

## Content
- 10 levels in 5 worlds: Harbor, Canyon, Autumn Woods, Frost Bay, Ember Peaks. Bosses: Goliath (L3), Ironclad (L5, L9), Wasp Queen (L7), and a double boss on L10.
- Gates: add, multiply/divide ladder, damage, fire rate, merge, trap, locked. Shots pass through and count once per gate.
- Weapons: Rifle, Shotgun, Minigun, Rockets, Laser (from crates or bought in the Arsenal).
- Enemies: runners, brutes, shielded, flyers, boss orbs. Hazards: explosive barrels, cryo tanks, barricades.
- Allies from breakable containers: Sentry Turret, Gunship, Ally Mech, Sniper Nest, Drone Swarm. Plus the Air Strike button and supply drops.
- Meta: coins, 10 upgrade tracks, 6 heroes (coins + boss medals), weapon shop, stars and best score per level, saved to `Save/gatebreaker.sav`.
- See FUN.md for the design notes and PROGRESS.md for the build log.

## Source layout
`src/*.lua` → `tools/build_lua.sh` → `project/Assets/Scripts/game.lua`. Assets are generated by `tools/gen_*.py` and cooked with `tools/cook_all.sh`. Balance is checked with `python3 tools/simtest.py <seed>` (headless sim of all 10 levels).
