# ArkanoidRemix — how the game works (read from current source)

Source of truth: `Arcade/ArkanoidRemix/Assets/Scripts/game.lua` (1179 lines). Byte-identical concatenation of
`Arcade/ArkanoidRemix/tools/src/0*.lua` via `tools/build_lua.sh:1-3` (verified). Cites are
`Assets/Scripts/game.lua` lines. Jev wrapper `Assets/Scripts/jev_auto.lua`; driver
`Assets/Scripts/gamekit/driver.lua`. `run.ps1` launches `Scenes/main.pescene` (`run.ps1:29-30`), which loads
`Scripts/game.lua`.

## Shape of a run

- Fixed step 1/60 s, at most 7 steps per rendered frame (`game.lua:153`, `1093-1102`).
- Logic field 13 wide × 15 tall: x ∈ [−6.5, 6.5], y 0 (bottom) to 15 (top wall) (`game.lua:70`, `443`).
- 33 rounds: rounds 1–32 are brick layouts, round 33 is the DOH boss (`game.lua:302-305`, `342-344`).
- New game: score 0, 3 lives, round 1, ball served (`game.lua:357-360`).
- Round phase machine: `ready` (0.8 s) → `play`; `dead` on losing the ball; `clear` when all breakable
  bricks are gone or the warp is used (`game.lua:348`, `525-531`, `541-543`, `629`).

## Player actions

- Move: A/D/arrows or mouse delta; paddle speed 13 units/s from keys, mouse adds up to ±1.5 per frame
  (`game.lua:535`, `1084-1092`).
- Paddle is a box of half-height 0.2 at y = 1.0; width starts 2.0 and lerps toward its target at 10/s
  (`game.lua:308-312`, `533`).
- Fire/launch: Space/Z/J/Up/W/left click (`game.lua:1071`). With the ball stuck, fire launches it; with
  laser active, fire shoots (`game.lua:548`, `555-557`).
- Launch angle: from the ball's offset on the paddle, `t*55°` from vertical, plus 25° if nearly centered
  (`game.lua:363-368`).
- Ball speed on serve: base speed, or 6.0 while Slow is active; base is `7.0 + speed_lv*0.55`
  (`game.lua:361`, `367`).
- Paddle hit: angle from vertical `t*62°` where t is the hit offset over half-width; ball speed is kept
  (`game.lua:478-490`).
- The ball is kept off a horizontal stall: if |vy| < 0.3·speed it is pushed to 0.3·speed vertically
  (`game.lua:494-496`).

## Hazards and what kills the player

- Losing every ball: a ball below y = −0.6 is removed; when none remain, `lose_vaus()` runs, phase `dead`,
  one life lost (`game.lua:552-553`, `500-504`).
- DOH shots: aimed at the paddle at speed 5.5; a hit within paddle half-width + 0.12 and half-height + 0.12
  kills the Vaus immediately (`game.lua:610-615`, `622-627`).
- Enemies that reach the paddle are destroyed and score 100; they do **not** damage the player
  (`game.lua:600`).
- Bricks, walls, the warp gate, gates and the backdrop do not damage the player. There are no ladders and
  no other lethal hazards.
- Death: after 2.2 s the paddle respawns if lives > 0; if lives ≤ 0 the run ends (`game.lua:517-523`).

## What the player can kill

- Bricks: every brick except gold (`X`). Gold only flashes and plays a hard sound (`game.lua:407-415`).
  Silver `S` has `2 + floor((round-1)/8)` hits (2 hits in rounds 1–8, up to 5 in rounds 25–32)
  (`game.lua:335`).
- Enemies: the ball or a laser shot destroys one for 100 points (`game.lua:471-476`, `568`).
- DOH: 16 ball hits (`game.lua:343`, `459-468`). Laser shots only spark off DOH and do no damage
  (`game.lua:569-570`).
- A round clears when `G.left` (all non-gold bricks) reaches 0 (`game.lua:337`, `629`).

## Bricks, rounds and scoring

- Brick cell 1.0 × 0.5, 13 columns; row 1 is at the top (`game.lua:310`, `323`).
- Brick values: W 50, O 60, C 70, G 80, R 90, B 100, P 110, Y 120; silver = 50 × round number
  (`game.lua:311`, `412`).
- Rounds 1–8 use hand-authored layouts (six rows, staircase, gold bars, columns, invader, gold pillars,
  diamond, gold maze); rounds 9–32 are generated by six style patterns; round 33 is DOH
  (`game.lua:256-303`, `342-344`).
- Gold `X` bricks are indestructible and never count toward `G.left` (`game.lua:337`, `408`).
- Extra Vaus: crossing score 20000, then every 60000 (20k, 80k, 140k, …) adds one life
  (`game.lua:316-321`).
- Hi-score loaded from `Save/arkanoid_hiscore.txt`; saved on game over only when Jev is not the assisted
  player (`game.lua:244-246`, `1017-1026`).

## Capsules

Dropped only when there is exactly one ball, no capsule already on the field, and an 18% roll; never from
a silver brick (`game.lua:373-380`, `414`). Weighted pick `CAP_ORDER[1+floor(rand()^1.25*7)]`; a picked P
becomes E 60% of the time and a picked B becomes L 50% of the time (`game.lua:372`, `376-378`).
Collection box: |x − paddle| < width/2 + 0.37 and |y − 1.0| < 0.37 (`game.lua:577`).

`apply_cap` (`game.lua:381-404`) always scores 1000 and clears the previous mode first:
- S Slow: ball speed set to 6.0 and speed level reset to 0 (`game.lua:386`).
- C Catch: the ball sticks on paddle contact instead of bouncing (`game.lua:387`, `489`).
- L Laser: paddle guns; fire spawns two shots if fewer than 4 are alive (`game.lua:388`, `555-557`).
- E Enlarge: paddle target width 3.0 (`game.lua:389`, `533`).
- D Disruption: splits the current ball into two more at ±0.45 rad (`game.lua:390-400`).
- B Break: opens a warp gate on the right wall; steering past the normal limit scores 10000 and clears
  the round (`game.lua:401`, `535-539`).
- P Extra Vaus: +1 life (`game.lua:402`).
Any new capsule ends the previous effect, and a caught ball is launched when the mode is replaced
(`game.lua:383-385`).

## Speed ramp

- `speed_lv` starts at 0 each round (`game.lua:345`).
- Hitting the top wall adds 2 speed levels (unless Slow is active) (`game.lua:445-447`).
- Every 12th paddle hit adds 1 level, capped at 11 (`game.lua:487-488`).
- Ball speed is `7.0 + speed_lv*0.55` (`game.lua:361`).

## Win and lose

- Win: destroy DOH in round 33 → 2.5 s later phase `clear` → since round ≥ `G.LAST` (33) the game sets
  `won` and `out_of_lives`, which ends the run in state `over` with the victory text
  (`game.lua:617-619`, `525-528`, `1104`, `862-863`).
- Lose: a ball is lost while lives ≤ 0 (`game.lua:519`).
- Enemies and bricks cannot end the run; only loss of the ball or a DOH shot can.

## JevAuto.attach for this game

Attach block `game.lua:1136-1174`, port 9138, id `arkanoid_remix`.

`observe()` = `observe_match()` returns exactly `paddle_x`, `ball_x`, `ball_y`, `ball_vx`, `ball_vy`,
`lives`, `score` (`game.lua:966-985`). The wrapper adds `mode`, `terminal`, `score`, `busy`, `actions`
(`jev_auto.lua:54-61`). `ball_*` describe the **lowest** ball, including a stuck one; with Disruption
active the other balls are not exposed (`game.lua:967-974`).

`busy()` is always false (`game.lua:1151-1153`).

Action ids produced by `actions()` (`game.lua:1156-1170`): `launch`, `right`, `left`, `stop`.
- Ball velocity is exactly 0 in both axes → `launch`.
- Else if `ball_x - paddle_x > 0.2` → `right`; `< -0.2` → `left`; otherwise `stop`.

`apply(id)` (`game.lua:951-964`):
- `left` / `right` set the held direction (and clear the other); the hold persists until the next act.
- `stop` clears both holds.
- `launch` queues a one-frame virtual Space pulse (`game.lua:906-930`).
- Unknown ids do nothing.
- `reset(seed)` seeds `math.random` and starts a match (`game.lua:997-1007`, `1172`); `on_human` clears
  all virtual keys and holds (`game.lua:890-904`, `1173`).

## Jev cannot see

- Round number and layout; how many breakable bricks remain (`G.left`), silver/gold distribution.
- The capsule on the field, its type, and whether one can drop.
- DOH: hp, position, flash, and every DOH shot in flight.
- Enemies on the field, their positions, and the gates they use.
- Ball count (multi-ball), each ball's `hold` timer, and the speed level.
- The active capsule mode (Slow/Catch/Laser/Enlarge), paddle target width, and laser shots in flight.
- Round phase (`ready`/`play`/`dead`/`clear`) and the respawn/clear timers.
- The warp gate state (`G.warp`) and that crossing it clears the round.
- Note the life count: `lives` here is the internal count including the current paddle; the HUD shows
  `lives - 1` reserves (`game.lua:839`, `982`).
